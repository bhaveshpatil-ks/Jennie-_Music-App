# Jennie Music - Artist Profiles Architecture

## Overview
Jennie Music implements a high-fidelity Spotify-style Artist Profile system that provides:
- Hero header with verified badge, live monthly listeners, and dynamic gradient overlays.
- Action bar with Play All, Shuffle Play, Follow toggle, and link sharing.
- Ranked Top 10 Popular tracks with duration and play counts.
- Discography categorization across Albums, Singles, and EPs.
- Full artist biographies and related artists recommendations.
