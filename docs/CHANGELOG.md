# Changelog - Jennie Music

## v2.0.0 (2026-09-29)
- **Spotify-Style Artist Profiles**: Complete profile pages with verified badge, monthly listeners, discography, and bio.
- **Dedicated Album Pages**: Full album tracklists, duration, and artist links.
- **Typo-Tolerant Search**: Aliases for all top artists (e.g. arjit singh -> Arijit Singh).
- **Synchronized Lyrics**: Line-by-line lyrics with tap-to-seek audio jumping.
- **Suspense Queue**: Hidden up-next card with swipe-to-reveal queue drawer.
- **Background Audio**: Zero throttling on mobile and background tab switches.
